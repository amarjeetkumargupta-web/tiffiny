## 4. Full Description (Under 4,000 Characters)
Tiffiny connects you with the best verified home kitchens, tiffin services, and regional mess providers in your neighborhood. Enjoy authentic, hygienic, homestyle Indian meals every single day with flexible subscription passes.

### Why Choose Tiffiny?

🍱 **Authentic Homestyle Food**
Say goodbye to oily, heavy restaurant food. Enjoy light, wholesome, home-cooked thalis, rotis, dals, and regional specialties prepared fresh daily with quality ingredients.

📅 **Smart Flexible Subscriptions (Monthly & Weekly Passes)**
Choose your meal frequency—Lunch only, Dinner only, or Both. Save up to 40% compared to ordering individual meals daily.

⏸️ **1-Click Meal Skip ("I'm Absent") with Auto Carry-Forward**
Heading out of town or having lunch with friends? Simply open the delivery calendar and tap "Mark Absent" before 9:00 PM the previous evening. Your missed meals are never wasted—they automatically extend your plan end date!

📍 **Hyperlocal Delivery**
Discover top-rated tiffin kitchens within your delivery radius. Real-time updates keep you informed as your meal is cooked, packed, and delivered on schedule.

💳 **Safe & Secure Payments**
Pay effortlessly using UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, or choose convenient monthly auto-renew mandates via Razorpay.

🥗 **Pure Veg, Jain & Regional Options**
Easily filter menus for 100% Pure Veg, Jain (No onion, no garlic), North Indian, Gujarati, South Indian, and Punjabi home kitchens.

### For Tiffin & Mess Kitchen Partners:
Are you a cloud kitchen, mess owner, or home tiffin caterer?
Join Tiffiny Partner Portal to streamline your daily kitchen operations:
- **Live Kitchen Prep Forecast**: Know exactly how many portions to cook each day with automatic deduction of customer skips.
- **Delivery Staff Dispatch**: Share 1-click WhatsApp run-sheets with your delivery boys—no complex apps needed.
- **Daily & Weekly Payouts**: Guaranteed timely settlements directly to your registered bank account.

Download Tiffiny today and bring the warmth of home-cooked meals to your daily routine!
